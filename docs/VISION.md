# SeoulMate Vision

## A private Korean travel companion

Version: 2.0

Status: Active

---

# Vision Statement

SeoulMate is a private digital travel companion created for a small group of friends traveling through South Korea.

It helps travelers prepare for their journey, enjoy their experience during the trip, and preserve their memories afterwards.

The core idea:

> Everything important for our Korean journey should exist in one beautiful, personal place.

---

# The Core Idea

Most travel applications focus on information management.

SeoulMate focuses on the experience of the journey.

It should not feel like:

- a spreadsheet
- a booking system
- a checklist
- a collection of external links

It should feel like:

- a personal travel companion
- a guide helping you navigate Korea
- a digital journal of an unforgettable experience

SeoulMate exists to reduce travel stress while increasing excitement and connection with the journey.

---

# Project Scope

SeoulMate is a private application created specifically for one group of travelers visiting South Korea.

The application is optimized for:

- approximately four users
- shared planning
- shared travel information
- shared memories

The project prioritizes:

- reliability over feature quantity
- simplicity over complexity
- real usefulness over theoretical possibilities

SeoulMate is not designed to become a public application, social network, or commercial product.

The goal is not maximum scale.

The goal is creating the best possible experience for this specific journey.

# Product Identity

SeoulMate has a strong Korean-inspired identity.

The application is inspired by:

- modern Seoul lifestyle
- Korean hospitality
- traditional Korean symbolism
- minimalist design
- warmth and friendliness

The goal is not to create a generic tourist application.

The goal is to create a personal experience that feels connected to Korea.

---

# Haechi Companion

## Emotional Identity

SeoulMate uses its own modern interpretation of Haechi (해치).

Haechi is inspired by the Korean mythical guardian creature associated with:

- protection
- guidance
- good fortune

Within SeoulMate, Haechi represents:

> A small companion that accompanies travelers through their Korean adventure.

Haechi is an emotional identity element of the application.

It should make the experience feel warmer and more personal.

Haechi is not a chatbot and does not replace application functionality.

---

# Korean Language Philosophy

The Korean dictionary is designed specifically for travelers.

The goal is practical communication, not academic language learning.

Every phrase should answer:

> "How can I actually say this in Korea?"

Each phrase should support:

## Hangul

The original Korean writing.

Example:

감사합니다

---

## Romanization

Standard written pronunciation.

Example:

Gamsahamnida

---

## Czech-Friendly Pronunciation

A pronunciation guide adapted for Czech speakers.

Example:

Kamsahamnida

The purpose is helping travelers pronounce Korean words naturally in real situations.

---

## Meaning

The practical meaning of the phrase.

Example:

Thank you.

---

Audio pronunciation may be considered if it can be implemented using free and reliable solutions.

# Product Principles

## 1. Companion Over Tool

SeoulMate should feel personal.

Features should support the feeling of traveling with a companion.

---

## 2. Beauty Improves Function

The application should be enjoyable and comfortable to use.

Design should improve clarity, confidence, and emotional connection.

---

## 3. Simplicity Over Complexity

The application should solve problems quickly.

Every feature should have a clear purpose.

---

## 4. Shared Journey

The trip belongs equally to all travelers.

Users should be able to contribute to:

- plans
- places
- notes
- memories
- shared information

---

## 5. Private By Design

SeoulMate is intentionally private.

It should not include:

- public profiles
- advertisements
- social networking features
- unnecessary data collection

---

## 6. Offline First

Travel situations may include unreliable internet access.

Important information should remain available offline whenever practical.

Examples:

- itinerary
- saved places
- Korean phrases
- essential travel information

---

## 7. Complete Over Perfect

A finished and reliable application is more valuable than unlimited features.

A smaller completed feature is better than a complex unfinished feature.

The project should avoid unnecessary expansion.

---
